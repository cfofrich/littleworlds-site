Tap a sticker, watch it play

Every sticker moves and makes a sound. The hen lays an egg that hatches. The puppy fetches the ball. The rocket blasts off and lands again. These are the real stickers from the app: give them a tap.

  • Nearly 100 stickers, and every one moves and makes a sound
  • Pinch to make stickers big or small, twist to turn them, and drag them back to the shelf to put them away
  • Stickers play together: animals eat, dogs bark, cranes lift, monkeys munch bananas
  • Soft background sounds, and the sun and moon turn day into night
  • Every world remembers your child’s picture for next time

Challenges for bigger kids

Tap the star in any world for “Can you…?” challenges, read aloud by a friendly recorded voice. Finish a round of 10 to win a prize sticker.

Challenges are optional and the words always stay on screen. Little ones can skip them and just play.

For grown-ups

Calm, safe screen time, made by a dad for his two little sticker fans.

  • Free, with no ads. Not now, not ever.
  • No in-app purchases. Every world is included.
  • No tracking or analytics. Nothing leaves the device.
  • No accounts and nothing to sign in to.
  • Works offline, in the car, on a plane, anywhere.
  • Built for Apple’s Kids category, with no links out of the app.
{{ui gear}}

Grown-up settings stay grown-up

Press and hold the gear for about a second to turn sounds, the voice, challenges and vibration on or off, or start fresh. A quick tap only shows a hint, so little fingers can’t wander in.

The app collects nothing. Read the privacy policy.
