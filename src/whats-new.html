What’s new

What has changed in Little Worlds: Stickers, newest first.

October 2026 Version 2.2

Now in Spanish, too

A new switch for bilingual families, and for anyone who wants their kids to hear some Spanish. Turn it on and the challenges, the sticker names and the speech bubbles are in Spanish, read by a warm native voice: “¡Haz que la vaca diga muu!”

  • It stays off until you turn it on. Press and hold the gear, then look under Voice.
  • With Say sticker names on too, each sticker’s name is said in Spanish: “Vaca” … “¡Muu!”
  • Everything else in the app stays in English, and you can switch back any time.
  • On small iPhones, a long challenge now always fits on its bar.

October 2026 Version 2.1

Say sticker names

A new switch in the grown-up settings. Turn it on to hear each sticker’s name, clearly and slowly, every time it’s tapped. Then the sticker makes its sound: “Cow” … “Moo!”

  • It stays off until you turn it on. Press and hold the gear, then look under Voice. The “Try it” button plays an example.
  • The grown-up settings are tidier, with a short line under every switch that says what it does.
  • On the home screen, the sun and the little world now do something when they’re tapped.

Say sticker names was a parent’s idea. Thank you!

October 2026 Version 2.0

A brand-new Little Worlds

The whole app, made again from the first sticker to the last.

  • Seven worlds, every one drawn fresh, including a friendly Halloween night.
  • Every sticker makes a sound and moves when it’s tapped.
  • Challenges read aloud by a friendly recorded voice, with prize stickers to win.
  • This website, where the stickers are the real ones from the app.
