Privacy policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Little Worlds: Stickers is designed for local, private play. We do not run ads, do not require accounts, and do not sell personal data.

Information we collect

  • We do not collect personal information from children or parents.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking SDKs, or third-party ad networks.
  • We do not collect location, contacts, photos, camera data, microphone data, or device identifiers for profiling.

How the app works

  • Gameplay and settings are handled on-device. The app works without an internet connection.
  • The pictures your child makes in each world, challenge progress and prize stickers won are saved only on the device, so they are still there next time. They are never sent anywhere. “Start fresh” in the grown-up settings deletes them; deleting the app deletes them too.
  • All sounds, background audio and the recorded voice that reads challenges aloud are built into the app. Nothing is downloaded or streamed, and the app never records or listens: it does not use the microphone. Grown-ups can turn each kind of sound off in the settings.
  • No account sign-in is required, and there are no in-app purchases or links out of the app.
  • No cloud backend is used.

This website

  • littleworlds.io has no cookies, ads, trackers or analytics tools, and it stores nothing on your device.
  • Everything on it (the fonts, pictures and sounds) comes from this site itself. Nothing is loaded from other companies.
  • Like every website, it is delivered by a hosting provider (Cloudflare), whose servers handle the technical details each visit carries, such as your IP address, to deliver the page and keep the site secure. Cloudflare also shows us overall traffic numbers, such as visits per day, counted on its own servers without cookies or scripts. We do not use any of this to identify or follow anyone.
  • The “Download on the App Store” button takes you to Apple’s App Store, where Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Children’s privacy

Little Worlds: Stickers is intended for young children and families. We intentionally avoid data collection and behavioral tracking.

Future changes

If we add diagnostics, analytics, or any new data collection in future versions, we will update this policy before release and provide any disclosures or consents required by law or platform rules.
